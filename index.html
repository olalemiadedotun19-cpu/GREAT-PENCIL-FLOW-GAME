RULES OF THE FLOW

The ball rolls automatically. Flows smoothly through a procedurally curving 3D track.
Swipe Left & Right to steer. Flick effortlessly across lanes on touchscreen or use A / D keys on desktop.
The pencil physically sketches your road! Watch the graphite tip draw ahead in real time, sketching upcoming turns, ramps, and hazards!
Dodge 20 stationery hazards. Avoid sharp pencil sharpeners, heavy rubber erasers, ink blots, drafting compasses, and blades.
Jump effortlessly at any speed! Tap anywhere on screen, swipe up, tap with a second finger while steering, or hit the large JUMP button. On desktop, press Space bar or W.
Collect Graphite & 2B Lead Boosts! Grab graphite shards along the road to unlock items in the Shop and supercharge your speed.
